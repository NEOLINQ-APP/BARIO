On the customer databases specifically — each invoice only charged $99.99 CAD for "installation & configuration." What actually happened per company was a dedicated private system, set up and debugged from scratch, plus 100+ real potential customers found with intro emails written, plus a built-in chat helper. A specialist who sets these systems up for a living typically charges $2,000–$4,000 per company just for that setup — before finding customers or building a chat helper. So each company is currently paying roughly 2–4% of what that work is actually worth.
